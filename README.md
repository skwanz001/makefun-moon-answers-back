One line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls
and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The line

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises. Not one of you
> ever wished me a good night back.

106 characters. The Moon has been listening for four billion years; tonight
it points out that nobody ever wished it back.

## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
