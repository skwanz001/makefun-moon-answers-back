One line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The line

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises. Not one of you ever wished me a good night back.

106 characters. The Moon has been listening for four billion years; tonight it points out that nobody ever wished it back.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (standard library only).
